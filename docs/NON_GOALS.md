# Non-Goals – What This Project Is NOT

## Explicit Non-Goals

### Statistics and Analytics (Not in MVP)
- **Note**: Statistics, analytics, and client tracking are NOT included in free plan
- Statistics dashboard is a Premium feature

### End Client Accounts (Not in MVP)
- **No**: No end client account required (booking without signup)
- **Future**: Optional end client accounts for booking history

### Social Media Login (Not in MVP)
- **No**: No social media login in MVP
- **Future**: OAuth integration (Google, Facebook)

### Booking Approval (Not Required)
- **No**: No booking approval required (automatic)

### Same-Day Booking Restriction
- **No**: No same-day booking restriction (configurable)

### Email Required (Not in MVP)
- **No**: No email required (optional)

### Address Collection (Not in MVP)
- **No**: No address collection in MVP

### SMS Fallback (Not in MVP)
- **No**: No SMS fallback in MVP
- **Future**: Email notifications as backup

### Usage-Based Pricing (Not in MVP)
- **No**: No usage-based pricing in MVP
- **Future**: Transaction fees for payment processing

### Client Database (Not in MVP)
- **No**: No client database in MVP (only in Premium plan)

### Recurring Appointments (Not in MVP)
- **Future**: Recurring appointments support

### Additional Service Types (Not in MVP)
- **Future**: Additional service types (hair coloring, styling, etc.)
- **Future**: Notes/special requests field

### Cancellation Fees (Not in MVP)
- **No**: No cancellation fees in MVP
- **Future**: Cancellation fees for late cancellations

### WhatsApp Reminder 2 Hours Before (Not in MVP)

### Per-Barber Timezone Configuration (Not in MVP)

### Currency Support (Not in MVP)
- **Future**: Currency support (for payments)

### Business Staff Role (Not in MVP)
- **Future**: Business staff role (STAFF) - limited permissions

### Multi-Barber Support (Not in MVP)
- **Note**: Multi-barber is a current feature but NOT included in MVP
- **How it works**: Admin (platform admin, not shop owner) can add multiple barbers within the same barbershop
- **Each barber has**: Individual calendar with independent schedules and availability
- **Setup**: Shop owner must contact admin to add a new barber/employee and set up their working hours
- **Future**: Full multi-provider support with shop owner self-service management

- **Future**: Shared resources (e.g., client database) across providers in same business
